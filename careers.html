HR Manager

HR & AdminKapilvastu, NepalFull-time

Apply Now →

About the Role

Dexin Manufacturing Nepal Pvt. Ltd. is seeking a capable and people-focused HR Manager to lead human resources and administrative activities and support a professional, compliant and productive workplace.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead day-to-day HR and administration activities across the organization.
  • Manage recruitment, onboarding, employee records and HR documentation.
  • Coordinate attendance, leave, performance management and employee-related processes.
  • Support training, employee development and workplace engagement initiatives.
  • Ensure HR practices are aligned with company policies and applicable Nepal requirements.
  • Work closely with department heads and management on workforce planning and employee matters.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Management, Business Administration or a related field.
  • Relevant experience in HR and administration, preferably in a manufacturing or corporate environment.
  • Strong communication, interpersonal and people-management skills.
  • Good knowledge of HR documentation, employee relations and workplace practices.
  • Ability to handle confidential employee information with professionalism and discretion.
  • Strong organizational skills and proficiency with standard office applications.

What We Value

We value integrity, teamwork, accountability, continuous learning and a practical approach to solving workplace challenges.
